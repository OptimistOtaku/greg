## Title

I Unplugged the Robot. Its AI Switched Bodies. | GREG

## Description

What happens when a robot fails halfway through a mission—and its knowledge
lives somewhere else?

Meet GREG: an AI crew powered by Gemini, MCP, and a shared FalkorDB knowledge
graph. In this simulated space station, three robots investigate connected
failures, share evidence, deliver a replacement part, and restore power. When
BOLT goes offline, its engineer agent transfers to ECHO and continues using the
crew's shared knowledge.

FalkorDB drives the decisions: which tasks are ready, which routes are open,
what connects the failures, and which replacement body can act.

Built for Graph Hacks 2026 — Track 01: Agents That Act on Connected Data.

Source code, setup, graph model, Cypher queries, and benchmark reports:
https://github.com/OptimistOtaku/greg

Demo download and captions:
https://github.com/OptimistOtaku/greg/releases/tag/v0.1.0-demo

This film is a time-compressed 3D reconstruction of recorded Gemini actions in
a synthetic environment. Includes synthetic narration and an original score.
Built with AI coding assistance; details in the repository. Thumbnail is
AI-generated promotional art inspired by the project.
