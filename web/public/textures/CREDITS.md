# Earth imagery

NASA Earth Observatory, The Blue Marble (2002).

Source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-blue-marble-true-color-global-imagery-at-1km-resolution/

- earth-day.jpg: Land Surface, Ocean Color, and Sea Ice.
- earth-clouds.jpg: Clouds.
- earth-night.jpg: Land, Ocean Color, Sea Ice, City Lights.

NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. Image by Reto Stöckli (land surface, shallow
water, clouds); enhancements by Robert Simmon (ocean color, compositing).
Additional data: MODIS teams, USGS, NOAA, Defense Meteorological Satellite Program.

These are public Earth visualization assets. The orbital scene is an artistic
simulation, not current satellite imagery or a physically scaled orbital model.
NASA does not endorse this project. No NASA insignia is used.
